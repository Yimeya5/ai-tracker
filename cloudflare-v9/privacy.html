Privacy policy

AI Pro · last updated November 6, 2026

Draft: to be checked by the owner before real payments go live.

Short version

No account, no login, no ads, and we don't sell your data.

What we keep

  • For free credits: a scrambled (hashed) version of your internet address (IP). We use it only to give each visitor their 10 free credits once and to stop abuse. The scrambled address can't be turned back into your real address. It is kept up to one year.
  • If you buy credits: your AI Pro code, how many credits are left, and the Stripe payment reference, so your credits work and a payment is never counted twice.
  • On your device: your settings (light/dark, which AIs you ticked) and your code if you bought credits, saved in your browser. You can delete them by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Your questions

When you use Ask, your question is sent to the AI companies you picked (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI) to get their answers. It is passed through a service called OpenRouter. AI Pro doesn't save your questions or the answers after they are shown to you. The AI companies handle the question under their own privacy rules. Files you attach in Explore or Race are read in your browser and sent with your question to the AIs you pick, only to get their answers; AI Pro doesn't save them. Please don't type or attach private information like passwords or ID numbers.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's privacy policy applies to the payment.

Hosting

The site runs on Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare may keep short technical logs (such as IP address and time) to keep the service running and secure.

No ads, no selling

We don't show ads, don't use advertising trackers, and don't sell or rent anyone's data.

Children

AI Pro is not made for children under 13.

Questions or deletion

Email yisroel9025@gmail.com. If you want your paid code's data deleted, send the code and we'll remove it (any unused credits on it will be lost).
